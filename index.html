Digital Forensics and Incident Investigation

When a security incident occurs, timely and methodical investigation is essential. Our digital forensics services help organizations understand:

  • What happened
  • When and how the incident began
  • Which systems or accounts may have been affected
  • Whether the incident spread further
  • What evidence should be preserved
  • Which steps can reduce the risk of recurrence
Threat modeling

Threat Modeling Before Problems Occur

Threat modeling helps organizations anticipate how systems could be attacked before an incident takes place. We map potential attack paths, identify critical assets, examine trust boundaries, and evaluate the controls intended to protect them.

This forward-looking approach helps teams prioritize security improvements while systems are being designed, developed, or changed—when fixes are often more effective and less costly to implement.

Evidence to action

From Finding to Fix: Reporting and Retesting

A security assessment is only valuable if its findings can be acted upon. At the conclusion of each engagement, we provide a detailed report that combines technical evidence with plain-language explanations of business risk.

Reports typically include:

  • A clear description of each finding
  • Severity and risk context
  • Evidence and reproduction details appropriate to the engagement
  • Potential business impact
  • Practical remediation recommendations
  • Prioritized next steps

Where requested, we also retest remediated findings to verify that identified vulnerabilities have been addressed and that corrective changes have not introduced new issues.

The result is not simply a vulnerability report—it is a documented, verified security improvement.
A responsible approach

Ethical hacking in action.

During an authorized web application assessment, our team discovers that a user with limited permissions can access an administrator-only page. Rather than exploiting the issue beyond what is necessary, we use a dedicated test account to reproduce the flaw, determine its scope, collect appropriate evidence, and document the steps clearly.

We then explain the technical and business implications, support the development team as it addresses the issue, and retest the application to confirm the fix.
